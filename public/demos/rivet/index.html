+Before we begin

A few good
questions.

Wondering where to start?
You’re in the right place.

Ask us something
01

What stage should our idea be at?

A question, a rough prototype, or a product that needs a new direction. We start by understanding what you’re trying to change, then agree the smallest useful next step together.

02

Can we work with you on design only?

Yes. We can shape a product, build a design system, or work alongside your engineers. The engagement is built around the help you need, with a clear set of deliverables.

03

How do you keep us involved?

We work in a shared project space, show progress each week, and leave room for considered feedback. You speak directly to the people doing the work.

04

Who owns the work at the end?

You do. The agreed deliverables include source files, the code we create for you, documentation, and a practical handover. Any third-party licenses are identified before we start.

05

What happens after launch?

We can plan a focused period of support, or continue as an embedded product partner. We agree the coverage, response times, and next priorities with you.

06

How do we get a proposal?

Send us a little context through the project page. We’ll arrange a conversation, understand your constraints, and come back with a scope, timeline, and fee you can review.
