Creative direction

Make room for a little personality.

By the studioSeptember 24, 20262 minute read
A little more you.:)

The part of your brand that feels most human is often the part you edit out. A little room for personality can make your content easier to recognize, and easier to care about.

Start with a point of view.

A point of view is more useful than a long list of adjectives. What do you notice that others miss? What do you care about enough to keep talking about? Give your creative team a clear answer, and they have something to make from.

Leave room for the person.

A creator brings more than a face to a brief. They bring a rhythm, a voice, and a relationship with their audience. Agree the essentials, then let them find the version of the story that feels natural to them. Direction should give the work a shape, rather than write out every breath.

Build a world, not a uniform.

Consistency can live in the color, the attitude, the edit, or the kinds of moments you choose. Every piece doesn’t need the same composition. A recognizable world has enough structure to hold together, and enough space for something unexpected.

Keep the interesting bit.

Before approving a piece, ask what you remember about it. If the answer is only the logo, there may be room for a stronger idea. The small, specific detail is often what makes a piece feel alive: a real reaction, an unusual observation, or a turn of phrase someone actually uses.
