Questions? Answers

Everything you need to know about the service and billing.

Will this hurt my domain?

No. We never send from your main domain. We set up separate, warmed domains and monitor their health every day, so your brand stays clean.

How is this different from a cold email tool?

A tool sends what you give it. We do the research, write each email, read every reply and book the call. You get meetings, not a dashboard.

Why do the emails feel so human?

Because a person researches each account and writes to it. Short, specific and with a single clear question.

Do I need to approve every email?

Only the first sequence. After you sign off on the voice and the offer, we send and report. You can review anything at any time.

Does it work with my CRM and calendar?

Yes. HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Google Calendar and Calendly, with the full email thread attached to every meeting.

Can I cancel if it is not for me?

Any time, with no contract. And if we do not book a qualified meeting in your first 14 days, your first month is free.

Is the list compliant?

We only contact business addresses, honour every opt-out immediately and follow the rules in each country we send to.
