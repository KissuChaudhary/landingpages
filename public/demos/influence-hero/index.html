Process

Raw clips in, viral content out.

Four steps, one monthly rhythm. You only ever do two things: send footage and approve.

  1. Day 0

    Kickoff call

    Thirty minutes on your goals, your audience and the topics only you can talk about.

  2. Every week

    Send us your footage

    Film on your phone and drop it in a shared folder. No special kit, no scripts needed.

  3. Within 3 days

    We edit. You approve.

    Hooks, captions, music and a cover for every video. Review in one place and hit post.

  4. Every month

    Track what works

    A one-page report on what grew your audience, so next month doubles down on it.

Every video includes

  • A tested hook
  • Live captions
  • Licensed music
  • A cover frame
  • A written caption
  • Posting times

Questions

Answers before you ask.

The things creators ask before booking a call.

What footage do I need to send?

Anything you can film on a phone: talking to camera, a walk-and-talk, behind the scenes. We turn it into finished videos.

Do I need to write scripts?

No. Send us the ideas you already talk about and we shape them into hooks and structure. You approve every video before it goes live.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Every finished video, caption and cover is yours to post anywhere and keep after you leave.

Which platforms do you edit for?

TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Snapchat Spotlight. Each video is exported at the right size for all four.

How fast will I see results?

Most creators see a lift in views within the first month and steady follower growth by month three. We share a report every month so you can see it.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes. Plans run month to month, or every three months at a discount. Pause or cancel with 14 days' notice.
