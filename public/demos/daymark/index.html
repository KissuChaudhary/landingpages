A few useful answers

Before we
say hello.

Good collaborations begin
with clear expectations.

Who is Daymark for?

Commerce and subscription brands with a considered product and an ambition to build lasting customer relationships. The best starting point is a conversation about your offer, team and next challenge.

Can we start with one discipline?

Yes. A focused acquisition, creative, retention or measurement brief can be a useful first step. We will agree on what is included and where your own team will take the work forward.

How are scope and fees agreed?

We define the deliverables, review points and responsibilities together. The clarity sprint has an indicative starting fee; ongoing partnerships are scoped around the work. Media spend and third-party production are agreed separately.

Do you promise a particular result?

We promise a considered process, clear responsibilities and honest reporting. Commercial outcomes depend on your category, offer, budget and market conditions. We agree on the measures that matter before starting.

What will our team receive?

The agreed strategy, editable creative files, journey maps and working notes. A considered handover gives your team the context to keep moving after a focused project.
