A few things, before we start

Glad you
asked.

01

What kind of brands do you work with?

Independent consumer brands, ambitious founders, and teams that want a more distinctive creative presence. A clear point of view matters more to us than the size of your business.

02

Can we start with one campaign?

Absolutely. A focused campaign is a great way to work together. We agree the direction, deliverables, schedule and fee before the project begins.

03

Do you work with our existing team?

Yes. We can become your creative partner or work alongside your in-house team, photographer or media agency. Roles and responsibilities are agreed at the outset.

04

How do feedback and approvals work?

We set clear review moments and bring you into the creative conversation early. Your proposal defines the feedback rounds and who signs off each stage.

05

What happens on our first call?

We talk about your brand, what you are trying to change, and what a useful partnership would look like. No presentation required—just your point of view.
