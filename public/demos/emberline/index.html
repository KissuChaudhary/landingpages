Which AI models do you track?

ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini. Growth and Scale track all four; Starter tracks two models of your choice.

How do you decide which prompts to track?

We analyse your site and your competitors to suggest the questions your buyers ask, and you can add your own at any time.

How often is the data refreshed?

Starter refreshes weekly. Growth and Scale refresh daily and send alerts the moment something changes.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, and we do not ask for a card.

Does it work outside English?

Yes. You can track prompts in more than 30 languages, and reports follow the language you choose.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from your settings in two clicks. You keep access until the end of the billing period.
