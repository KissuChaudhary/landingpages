Every section knows the last.

Most tools write an article in one pass and lose the thread by section five. Footnote drafts recursively: each section is written with everything before it in view, so the argument builds instead of drifting.

Questions

The fine print.

Short answers to what teams ask before they start.

What is GEO?
Generative engine optimization: writing so that AI answer engines choose your page as a source. It rewards primary data, clear structure and claims a model can verify.
Which engines does it target?
ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude. We track citations across all four and report changes weekly.
Does it replace our editors?
No. It replaces the first draft and the fact-checking pass. Your editors decide what is worth publishing.
Where do the sources come from?
Live pages, read at the time of writing. Every citation links to the page it came from, and nothing is quoted from memory.
Can it match our brand voice?
Yes. Point it at your ten best posts and it builds a voice profile. You can edit the profile at any time.
How does the trial work?
Fourteen days, three full articles, no card. If you stop, your drafts and citations stay yours.
