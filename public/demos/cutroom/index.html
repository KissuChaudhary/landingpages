Scene 06/FAQ

Questions before you start.

If yours is not here, send it with your first upload and we will answer it there.

Q01How fast do I get my first video?

Long-form videos come back in 48 hours from upload and Shorts in 24. Rush delivery is available on request.

Q02Will the edit match my style?

Yes. In the first week your editor builds a style guide from your best videos: pacing, fonts, sound, humour. Every cut after that follows it.

Q03How many revisions are included?

Two rounds on every video. Most videos need one, and you can leave notes right on the timeline.

Q04Do you handle thumbnails and titles?

Yes. Long-form videos include three thumbnail options and a title suggestion. Both are optional extras on Shorts.

Q05What footage and tools do you need from me?

Just a link to your raw footage and a few notes. We work with any camera and any format, and you do not need to install anything.

Q06Can I pause or cancel?

Any month. There is no contract, and unused videos roll over for 30 days.
