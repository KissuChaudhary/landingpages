THE INTENDED CHANGE

Choose the questions before the dashboard

The most useful operational brief answers the questions a team already asks. This concept connects a small set of agreed sources and organizes updates around those decisions.

Show what changed and why it matters

The brief separates observed changes from interpretation. Each item links to its source, includes the relevant period and makes assumptions explicit, giving a reviewer something concrete to check.

A starting point for judgment

A summary cannot replace the context of the people doing the work. The reviewed brief is an input to discussion, with uncertain items turned into questions rather than confident conclusions.
