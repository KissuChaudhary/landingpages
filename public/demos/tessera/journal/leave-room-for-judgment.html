HUMAN OVERSIGHT / 3 minute read

Good automation leaves room for judgment.

A human approval is most useful when the person has enough context to make a real decision.

An approval is a design problem

Asking someone to click a button is not the same as giving them oversight. Show what is proposed, which information informed it and what the action will change. Make editing or rejecting the proposal straightforward.

Choose meaningful boundaries

Different tasks need different levels of autonomy. Document the permitted actions and the situations that require review. Keep those boundaries visible to the people who operate the system.

Learn from the corrections

Review decisions can reveal where instructions, sources or workflow assumptions need attention. Agree on a practical way to evaluate those corrections and improve the system over time.
