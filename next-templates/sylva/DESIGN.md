# Sylva design notes

Positioning: considered botanical interiors for everyday living. The original copy centres on light, scale, placement and practical care instead of a generic plant shop.

The supplied Botanica reference was inspected live on desktop, including its large hero specimen, organic approach panel, pinned spreading-card collection, scroll-revealed benefits around a central plant, editorial chapters and closing. Its layout rhythm informs Sylva; no code or assets were copied.

Sylva has its own brand mark, forest/lichen palette, Instrument Serif/DM Sans type system, three distinct plant personalities, four original interior/nursery photographs, dedicated plant and space pages, and reviewed project briefs. The reference's elongated scroll holds are shorter, the collection links are real routes, and phone/reduced-motion views read in normal flow.

The hero specimen belongs to one `hero-journey` coordinate space. Its position scales down as it travels into the nursery panel. Three plant cards use scroll progress to unfold from a centred deck. Focus expands all cards immediately. A central care specimen grows subtly as its four principles enter view. There are no continuous loops or pretend application screens.

Display type remains generous, controls stay compact, photo masks are organic, and pale line work is used sparingly. The gallery alternates a tall home image with two wider workplace/hospitality scenes. Preserve hierarchy and image proportions when customizing.
