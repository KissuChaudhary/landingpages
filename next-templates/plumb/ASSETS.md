# Assets

Plumb ships no photographs. Apart from the product screens, everything you see is code:

- **Product screens** (`public/images/*.webp`): original interface screens designed for this template, rendered from HTML at 2× and saved as WebP with sample figures. Replace them with screenshots of your own product.
- **Plumb mark**: an original SVG (a plumb line and its bob) in `components/ui/Brand.tsx` and `public/icon.svg`.
- **Customer wordmarks**: fictional products set in the template's own fonts in `components/ui/Logos.tsx`. They are not real brands.
- **Signature**: one hand-drawn SVG stroke in `components/sections/Letter.tsx`.
- **Avatars**: initials, generated from each name.
- **Icons**: original line icons drawn inline (the feature grid, buttons and the screens).

## Fonts

Loaded and self-hosted at build time through `next/font/google`:

- **Funnel Display** and **Funnel Sans**, SIL Open Font License 1.1
- **Geist Mono**, SIL Open Font License 1.1

## Content

The brand, people, sites, quotes, figures, plans and product claims are fictional. Replace them before launch.
