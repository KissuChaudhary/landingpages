# Asset provenance

All Tempo product visuals were created in this project as editable HTML, CSS and SVG: the physical focus dial and its ticks, device frame, app icon and mark, app screens, paper notes, morning sun, focus wave, reflection sheet, weekly circles, pace rings and leaf drawing. No purchased graphics, stock photography or generated raster assets are included.

DM Sans and Lora are supplied through Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. Geist Mono is also SIL Open Font License. `next/font` self-hosts fonts after build. Lucide interface icons use the ISC license. Third-party licenses remain applicable independently of the commercial template license.

Marketplace screenshot previews are browser captures of this implementation. App names, people, quotes, routine, membership features and prices are fictional demonstration content. The sample week is illustrative, not a measured user activity history.
