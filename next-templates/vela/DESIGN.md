# Vela design direction

Design system reference: a warm, relationship-centric SaaS aesthetic with a saturated opening, centered headline, fine diagonal field, large white dashboard, staggered metric cards, broad stacked product panels, framed page rhythm, team stories, three plans, split FAQ and a warm closing.

Retained: the overall editorial rhythm and motion cues — load reveals, counters, scroll-staggered cards, transitioning product chapters, moving diagram signals, rolling button labels, pricing selection and disclosures. Vela uses quicker early reveals to avoid blank sections during fast scrolling.

Original direction: a relationship workspace for growing service teams, centered on account context, owned follow-ups and renewals. Manrope replaces the serif. Coral, espresso, sage and lilac carry the story. All visuals and copy are original source assets. Marketing interactions lead to destinations or run in place; no modal flows.

Integration: standalone Next.js project in next-templates/vela. npm run export:demo builds public/demos/vela for the Hairline UI iframe and catalog. Buyer documents omit these maintainer procedures.
