# Aveniq design brief

Reference: https://zridora.framer.website/ — inspected on 10 October 2026.

The reference uses a black canvas, a full gradient split hero with an AI chat illustration, six uniform features, a sticky three-step process, four overlapping case-study cards, integration logos, three plans, animated result numbers, a testimonial slider, FAQs and a gradient closing. Text reveals from blur; cards and process content take focus through scroll.

Aveniq keeps the luminous dark technology atmosphere and restrained scroll storytelling while changing the identity, positioning, imagery, illustrations and composition. It introduces a fictional product decision workspace, a dark left hero with a cooler sculpture field on the right, a contextual decision brief rather than chat, a practical principle strip, three unequal diagram-led capabilities, a two-column process with an orbit schematic, three use-case cards with reversed copy/image composition, a context constellation, monthly/yearly plans, a two-column FAQ, a luminous closing and an oversized footer wordmark.

Three original generated glass sculptures replace reference media. SVG identity, product diagrams, copy and layout are original. No invented ratings, testimonials, business results, compliance badges or customer logos.

Landing page scope only: ordinary CTA destinations, in-place pricing and native FAQs. No form, signup implementation, dialog, detail route, download or backend. Buyer content and destinations are centralized in site.config.ts. Components and styles remain focused.

Maintain the catalog demo with npm run export:demo. This file, QA.md and the export helper are excluded from buyer delivery.
