# Included assets

The Tandem wordmark and interlocking mark are original designs included as editable SVG and typography.

All thirteen WebP product illustrations are original vector compositions created for this template: a project workspace, six specialist outputs, three workflow chapters, and three audience stories. They use fictional tasks and sample content, with no copied product screenshots or third-party photographs. Total image size is about 331 KB.

The live point sculpture is original procedural artwork. Its torus-knot geometry, perspective projection, depth shading, cursor response, and animation are defined in `components/motion/AgentSculpture.tsx`. The decorative orbit lines and integration map are CSS and SVG.

Mona Sans and Fragment Mono are provided through Google Fonts using `next/font`. Mona Sans is by GitHub and Fragment Mono by Wei Huang. Both use the SIL Open Font License; their upstream font files and license information are at [Mona Sans](https://github.com/github/mona-sans) and [Fragment Mono](https://github.com/weiweihuanghuang/fragment-mono).

Lucide icons are provided by `lucide-react` under its ISC license. Names of third-party tools in the illustrative integration section belong to their respective owners and do not imply a partnership or endorsement.
