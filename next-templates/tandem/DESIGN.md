# Tandem design notes — internal

The brief: a new AI agents landing template at Shear's level of motion and design craft, in light mode. Build an original identity and section hierarchy rather than recoloring Shear.

Tandem pairs human ambition with specialist AI backup. White, cool grey, and electric blue keep the page light throughout, including the footer. Mona Sans provides expressive scale and a width axis for the word transitions. Fragment Mono marks small coordinates and section metadata.

The hero has a bespoke canvas torus-knot sculpture. Points rotate in perspective and respond gently to the pointer. Research, creative, and operations cards orbit it; the title trades backup, headroom, and momentum. Both canvas instances rest off screen, when the tab is hidden, and under reduced motion.

Sections: asymmetrical hero; centered manifesto with three rolling figures; perspective-settling workspace; six-card specialist carousel; an expandable three-step workflow; three audience tabs with directional image/copy transitions; an SVG connection map; a human-approval editorial section; three plans with rolling prices; a sticky FAQ introduction; an email closing; a giant staggered scroll-driven wordmark.

Thirteen original SVG compositions were rasterized with `work/tandem-art.cjs`. Product UI ships as optimized images, following the existing template convention. No invented customer logos, testimonials, or performance results. The illustrations are identified as demo content; all product claims must be adapted before a buyer launches.

Maintain source in focused section components and separate stylesheets. No animation dependency. Existing Hairline UI components are shipped unchanged.
