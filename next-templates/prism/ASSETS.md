# Prism artwork provenance

Six original demo artworks were generated with OpenAI's built-in ImageGen tool for this project. They illustrate possible creative outputs; they are not outputs of a functioning Prism service. No third-party brand assets or real customer portraits are used. The editorial portrait depicts a fictional adult.

Included WebP images have 1200 × 1200 main files and 480 × 480 `-small` thumbnails, converted with Pillow. Conversion changed size and encoding only. Use the main image for full previews and the small image for cards. Source PNGs remain in the generation folder; the template is self-contained and does not require those external paths.
