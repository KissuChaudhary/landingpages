# Original assets

The three images were created for this template with the built-in imagegen tool on October 9, 2026. No image, logo, code or text was copied from Agentlab. The reference informed layout, typography, contrast and pacing.

Final shipped assets:

- `public/images/glass.webp` — cobalt glass architecture, 1536 × 1024.
- `public/images/portrait.webp` — editorial photograph of a fictional engineer, 1024 × 1536.
- `public/images/mesh.webp` — cobalt point-cloud waves, 1536 × 1024.

The generated PNGs were encoded to WebP without compositional edits. The original PNGs are retained in the development workspace at `work/conduit-art/`; only the optimized files are needed to run the template. Alt text identifies the artwork and person without implying a real customer endorsement.

## Final prompts

**Glass — built-in imagegen**

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original background artwork for a sophisticated white AI workflow landing page called Conduit. Create a panoramic 3:2 high-end architectural 3D render of enormous translucent cobalt-blue glass conduits and thick folded rectangular ribbons, interlocking through space. Perspective close-up, electric ultramarine blue #1746ff, pale ice-blue refractions, crisp diagonal geometric edges, glass planes extending beyond the frame, dramatic but clean studio lighting. Smooth spacious central area suitable for a small white interface overlay, extraordinary rich material detail along the perimeter. Minimal precise digital architecture, artistic glass sculpture, no round blobs, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermarks. Landscape high resolution.

**Portrait — built-in imagegen**

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original editorial portrait for sophisticated AI automation website Conduit. A candid young female software engineer with dark chin-length hair wearing a crisp white shirt, in side profile looking thoughtfully at a computer screen just outside the left edge. Waist-up portrait in a quiet modern studio. Cinematic cool ice-blue ambient light and subtle warm coral light on one cheek and hair edge, background clean desaturated cyan. Shot on 85mm editorial portrait lens, authentic skin texture, refined composition, premium technology magazine photography, contemplative human presence. Portrait orientation 2:3. No text, no logos, no numbers overlaid, no watermark, no glowing futuristic glasses, no exaggerated smile.

**Mesh — built-in imagegen**

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original abstract analytics background for a refined cobalt-blue technology website. A rolling, sculptural fabric surface made of thousands of tiny luminous ice-blue points on deep ultramarine, smoothly flowing in two broad shallow waves. Macro architectural perspective, a crystalline point-cloud mesh, precise fine dots, soft atmospheric depth, sophisticated restrained rendering. Clear brighter center upper area suitable behind a small white product UI. Electric cobalt #1746ff and icy periwinkle blue, no purple, no circular spheres, no text, no UI, no logos, no watermarks. Landscape 3:2 composition, high resolution.
