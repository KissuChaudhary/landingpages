# Design decisions

The requested reference is Agentlab, https://agentlab.framer.ai/. Conduit follows its composition and rhythm closely, with original branding, copy, diagrams and artwork. The layout stays centered in the hero and alternates open white chapters with dense black chapters. It does not borrow the split-hero pattern of other templates in this marketplace.

## Hierarchy

Merriweather 300 carries display headings. Inter carries the reading and control layers. Desktop headings use 56px, 44px and 25–32px; phone headings use 36px, 30px and 24–27px. Main paragraphs remain 14–16px. Smaller typography belongs to product screenshots and secondary metadata. One restrained cobalt label introduces each main heading. Controls share a square 44px foundation; only the compact navigation action is shorter.

## Structure

The frame has a shared gutter and one dashed rule on each side. Every section uses that same frame instead of drawing independent decorative lines. Small square markers sit exactly at the top corners where horizontal and vertical rules meet. Inner grids use single borders, with breakpoint-specific removal of redundant borders. Dark chapters extend to the viewport edge while preserving frame alignment.

The hero visual is a roughly two-to-one composition: a working agent selector over original glass architecture, and an editorial human photograph. Phones retain the functional agent scene. The product chapter uses three alternating rows with blue stages and one purposeful interface per stage. The analytics stage uses a distinct original point-cloud surface.

The remaining chapters preserve the reference's hierarchy: flow graphic, dark capability bento, sector grid, tabbed three-part stories, three circular control seals, a split FAQ, a two-column dark closing and a large pixel wordmark. Pricing stays on a separate page and uses three aligned plans.

## Motion

Section entrances move 18px and settle within 700ms. The problem statement resolves from gray to white, the impact line draws through the stages, bars rise from their baseline, and button text rolls on hover. Glass movement is slow. The task rails pause on hover. The footer pixel sparks remain subtle.

No smooth-scroll library or scroll hijacking is used. The system reduced-motion preference turns off entrance and ambient motion. A remembered footer preference controls ambient effects. User-triggered product runs still complete because their progress communicates the chosen action.

## Honest examples

The fictional stories describe the local blueprints rather than claiming real customer results. The impact graphic counts the actual four stages and the human decision. Control seals represent access, data and oversight principles rather than claiming third-party certifications. A buyer can replace all of this with verified product claims.
