# Assets and provenance

- The linked-ribbon mark, favicon, workspace, four feature illustrations and three example studies are original SVG/HTML/CSS created for Relay.
- No code, logos, screenshots, fonts or illustration files were copied from the referenced CodeForge and SkyAgent websites. The floating navigation, centered hero, blue light and joined grid are an original interpretation of the reference's broad compositional principles.
- DM Sans is loaded through `next/font/google` and distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Lucide icons retain their ISC license. Other dependencies retain their upstream licenses.
- The sample product, scenario content and prices are fictional. The calendar and reading context are prepared examples, with no connection to external services.
- Marketplace images are screenshots of the actual production export, resized for catalog, detail, phone and social previews.

No external photography, generated raster artwork or stock-image accounts are required. Replace the browser icon, mark and optional screenshot with your own brand assets before launch.
