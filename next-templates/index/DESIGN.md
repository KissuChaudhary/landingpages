# Index design system

The composition follows one idea: scattered information becoming organized understanding. The Infisical homepage informed the general use of assembled heading accents and brief label decoding; no branding, layout, source code or artwork was copied.

Warm white `#f7f6f2`, ink `#171916`, stone `#e9e7e1`, persimmon `#ed6a46`. Instrument Sans carries both display and reading. Geist Mono is reserved for short labels and source metadata. Hero 76px, section 44px, feature 24px, body 18px; mobile hero 44px and section 32px. Product screens have their own compact hierarchy. Buttons are 48px; navigation controls 40px inside a 64px row.

One badge precedes each main section heading. Shared gutters and rules express real section or interface boundaries. A wide source-to-synthesis scene leads into an open statement, one sticky product sequence, a dark evidence chapter, open example rows, three aligned plans, disclosures and a persimmon closing field. Product objects have boundaries; prose does not acquire decorative card wrappers.

Heading accents assemble in six bands over 550ms, staggered by 38ms. Hover can replay with a 1.4-second cooldown. Label decoding finishes in 300ms. Copy enters over 350ms with 8px travel. Hover feedback takes 200ms. Ambient fields move slowly in sparse steps; mouse response stays within four pixels each way. Source connection endpoints are measured from the rendered source and synthesis objects. ResizeObserver keeps the paths attached when text or dimensions change.

Native scroll remains intact. IntersectionObserver selects a story stage as its chapter enters the reading area; manual controls select and bring that chapter into view. Phones read all three illustrated chapters without pinning. System reduced motion and a remembered pause switch leave a complete static page with all controls working.
