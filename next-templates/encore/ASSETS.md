# Assets

Encore ships no photographs. Apart from the images below, everything you see is code:

- **Emails, dashboards and service previews** (`public/images/*.webp`): original designs made for this template, rendered from HTML at 2× and saved as WebP. The stores, products and figures in them are fictional. Replace them with your own work.
- **Encore mark**: an original SVG (a loop arrow in a rounded square) in `components/ui/Brand.tsx` and `public/icon.svg`.
- **Client names**: fictional brands set in type. They are not real companies.
- **Avatars**: initials on solid discs, generated from each name.
- **Circled phrase, timeline, rings**: SVG and CSS drawn at runtime.

## Fonts

Loaded and self-hosted at build time through `next/font/google`:

- **Hubot Sans** (variable weight and width), SIL Open Font License 1.1
- **Azeret Mono**, SIL Open Font License 1.1

The rendered images also use Geist, Geist Mono, Instrument Sans and Space Grotesk (all SIL Open Font License 1.1); they are part of the pictures, not loaded by the page.

## Icons

Interface icons come from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), ISC License.

## Content

The studio, founder, people, client brands, quotes, figures and prices are fictional. Replace them before launch.
