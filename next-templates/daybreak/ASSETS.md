# Original assets

All six raster images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this template. Generated PNG originals remain in `work/daybreak-art/` in the development workspace; the reusable template ships optimized WebP files in `public/images/`.

Landscape illustrations are 1536 × 1024. Portraits are optimized to 640 × 960. Encoding and resizing use Sharp; scene contents were not composited or altered. The portraits depict fictional people.

| Asset     | Saved path                     | Purpose                                       |
| --------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Hero      | `public/images/hero.webp`      | White-space hillside hero and closing section |
| Landscape | `public/images/landscape.webp` | Full-bleed workspace backdrop                 |
| Mara      | `public/images/mara.webp`      | Forma example perspective                     |
| Elliot    | `public/images/elliot.webp`    | Sundial example perspective                   |
| Noa       | `public/images/noa.webp`       | Northline example perspective                 |
| Leo       | `public/images/leo.webp`       | Fieldwork example perspective                 |
| Dashboard | `public/images/dashboard.webp`, `dashboard-phone.webp` | The workspace preview: rendered from the template’s own interface (desktop and phone layouts) |

The sunrise brand mark, fictional company marks, integration icons, charts and diagrams are editable SVG, CSS and React. Lucide icons retain their ISC license. Inter is self-hosted by Next.js at build time and retains its SIL Open Font License. Third-party dependencies retain their respective licenses.

## Final prompt set

### hero

-

+Create an original premium editorial gouache illustration for a calm AI marketing website called Daybreak. Wide horizontal 1536 by 1024 composition. The UPPER HALF of the entire image must be clean pure WHITE negative space, with no clouds, no texture, no objects, to seamlessly meet a white webpage. All landscape concentrated in the LOWER HALF: a sunlit Mediterranean hillside beside a distant sea, two small white artist studios with burnt-orange roofs, slender dark cypress trees, wildflower meadow in ochre, sage, coral and soft blue, a winding footpath and layered blue-green hills toward the horizon. More landscape detail at the left and right edges; the lower center has a quiet patch of meadow where a white interface panel will sit. Sophisticated hand-painted brush texture and confident cut-paper shapes, charming but grown-up, vivid restrained color, balanced painterly composition. Soft dry gouache edges dissolve into the pure white background along the top horizon. No mountains with snow, no people, no text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark, no UI. This must be a finished bespoke illustration, not a generic vector scene. +

### landscape

-

+Create an original wide horizontal 1536 by 1024 editorial gouache painting for the Daybreak software website. A warm sunrise over a coastal valley, sage and turquoise hills, a winding pale river, ochre grasses, coral and dusty-rose flowers in foreground, two tiny white architectural studios on the far right with terracotta roofs, tall deep green cypress trees. Sky occupies top third, very pale cream almost white, with soft blue haze. The center is calm and spacious and the painting will frame a large white software dashboard. Premium magazine illustration, sophisticated hand-painted brush and paper grain, confident graphic shapes with natural detail, beautiful soft morning light. Palette blue, sage, golden ochre, burnt-orange, white and muted rose. No people, no text, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no borders. Cohesive with a Mediterranean hillside gouache hero, but a different full-bleed landscape composition. +
