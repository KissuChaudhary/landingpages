# Inlay assets

Every picture in `public/images/` is original to this template. The posters, album covers, type specimens, halftone prints, plotter drawings, product screens and creator pages were designed for Inlay in HTML and SVG and exported as WebP. The people, brands, creators, venues and figures shown are fictional. Replace them with your own work and screenshots of your own product before presenting them as real.

The logo mark, favicon, icons and avatars are drawn in SVG for this template (`components/ui/Brand.tsx`, `components/ui/Icons.tsx`, `components/ui/Avatar.tsx`, `public/icon.svg`). The platform marks in the Connect section (`components/ui/Platforms.tsx`) are simplified single-colour drawings of third-party trademarks, shown only to say those platforms can be embedded; they belong to their owners.

Font: Mona Sans, under the SIL Open Font License, loaded through `next/font/google`.
