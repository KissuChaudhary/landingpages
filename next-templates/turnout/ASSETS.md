# Turnout assets

Every photograph in `public/images/` is original to this template. They were generated with OpenAI GPT Image 2.5 on 9 October 2026 and converted to WebP. The people, brands, venues and events shown are fictional. Replace them with your own photography before presenting them as your work.

The logo mark, favicon, client wordmarks and icons are drawn in SVG for this template (`components/ui/Brand.tsx`, `components/ui/Logos.tsx`, `components/ui/Icons.tsx`, `public/icon.svg`).

Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque and Geist, both under the SIL Open Font License, loaded through `next/font/google`.

Every photo prompt shared one direction: contemporary lifestyle campaign photography, candid, direct on-camera flash mixed with natural light, saturated true-to-life colour, accents of chartreuse lime and periwinkle blue in clothing or set design, no text, logos or watermarks.
