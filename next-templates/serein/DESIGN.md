# Serein design direction

Design system reference: an editorial studio landing-page system with an autoplaying monochrome ribbon video, rotating project miniatures, individually revealed text, project cards that stack and shrink on scroll, expanding service surfaces, native-style FAQs and dark/light chapter changes. The original uses very large lowercase lettering, rounded image corners, quiet gray rules and a fixed black header.

Serein preserves that editorial structure while introducing an independent brand-and-digital studio, original portfolio campaigns, a neutral paper palette, a soft citron accent and its own complete content. The backdrop is an original image displaced by a small WebGL shader rather than the reference video. Reveals complete within 850ms; they do not gradually type long paragraphs. The portfolio consists of three coherent case studies, with real routes and editable identity boards. No fabricated client metrics or testimonials were introduced.

Buyers receive small section components, scoped style files, typed content, true contact/booking destinations, static-host URL support and local assets. No marketing modal interactions.
