The creator portrait and three concept campaigns are illustrative; they do not show real client engagements. Replace the work gallery with your own projects and update its labels before presenting it as a client portfolio.

Keep the portrait vertical and the campaign images horizontal when replacing them. All files are local and need no image-service credentials.

## Fonts and marks

Space Grotesk and Inter are bundled as Latin variable WOFF2 files in `public/fonts/` and loaded through `next/font/local`. Their upstream SIL Open Font Licenses are included beside the files. To support additional writing systems, replace the font files with suitably licensed subsets and update the two source paths in `app/layout.tsx`. The brand mark, starburst and service drawings are editable SVG components. The texture is a small inline SVG filter. No third-party icons or stock-photo URLs are required.
