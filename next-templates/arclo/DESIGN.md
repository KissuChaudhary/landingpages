# Design

Autonomus informed the composition: a compact navigation that contracts on scroll, a centered two-line headline over a luminous textured gradient, a bordered workflow canvas, a quiet logo strip, three benefits, a three-plus-two feature grid, a central framework, three plans, four onboarding views, team stories, split about section, journal, split FAQ and gradient closing panel. Arclo preserves that visual sequence with its own brand, original copy, diagrams and photography.

Manrope is the only typeface. Desktop hero display is 52px at weight 800; main section headings are 44px at weight 750, cards 20px at weight 650, body 15–16px. Phone display is 36px and section headings 32px. Fine rails, actual intersection dots, inset surfaces and a restrained 44px control size keep the composition coherent. Purple and coral are concentrated in the hero and controls; smaller product surfaces carry quiet tinted details.

The five feature scenes each explain a distinct function. The live workflow canvas shares its data and execution with a larger explorer. No static product screenshots or source-site assets are used. Story result language illustrates qualitative benefits without invented numerical performance claims. Pricing retains clear totals and separate monthly/yearly destinations.

Native scrolling, small transform reveals, hover feedback and finite workflow steps provide motion. Decorative transitions respect the system setting and a remembered manual pause. Reveals keep content visible from the first render. Phones use a compact menu, a readable two-by-two workflow layout, stacked feature/plans, four vertically arranged onboarding controls, one story at a time and complete stacked forms.
