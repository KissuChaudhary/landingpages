# Assets

`public/images/team.webp` is an original image made with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-09. The generated 1536×1024 PNG was encoded as WebP at quality 86 without changing the composition. The people are fictional. CSS crops from the same photograph provide the illustrative avatars and story portraits.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium SaaS landing page about-section photograph. Create an original candid editorial photo of a small fictional product team of four people, two women and two men with varied ethnic backgrounds, late twenties to forties, working together at a pale oak table in a beautiful bright creative studio. A woman in a charcoal sweater explains a paper flowchart, the others lean in thoughtfully, one laptop, coffee cups, a small violet glass vase. Soft northern daylight, white walls, chrome chairs, tasteful quiet plants, slight analog grain, refined natural colors, warm professional mood, beautiful photographic composition. Wide landscape 3:2 framing. Real skin texture, unposed and authentic, excellent natural hands. No typography, no logos, no watermarks. These are entirely fictional people.
