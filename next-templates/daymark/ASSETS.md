## sola

Prompt:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: original product campaign photography for a polished growth studio website. Create a premium photorealistic skincare still life for the fictional brand 'sola'. A tall soft lime-green cylindrical pump bottle and a matching short jar sit on a brushed stainless steel platform, against a pale periwinkle-lilac studio background. Exact small black packaging text: 'sola' and 'daily care'. Strong but soft late-afternoon sunlight from the left, elegant diagonal shadows, restrained editorial art direction, beautiful material detail, no water splash. Portrait 4:5 framing, product group centered and generously surrounded by negative space; the composition must crop well to a square. This is a real campaign photo, not a UI screenshot or a website mockup. No additional text, no watermarks, no famous brand shapes.
