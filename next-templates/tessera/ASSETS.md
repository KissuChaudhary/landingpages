# Assets

All geometric illustrations are original code-native artwork included with the project. The isometric sculpture is generated from SVG polygons in `components/art/Sculpture.tsx`. Workflow diagrams, symbols, the brand mark, article compositions and favicon use local SVG and CSS. There are no external stock photographs, reference-site assets, videos or remote textures.

Geist and Geist Mono Latin font files are bundled in `public/fonts/`. Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their original license notices are included beside the WOFF2 files. Font files were obtained from the official Google Fonts distribution of the Geist Project.

All studio copy, example system narratives and article copy were written for this project. The studio and engagements are fictional. Replace them with accurate content for your business before launch.
